# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to **BouncyBall**, developed by **dududev**.

We respect your privacy. BouncyBall does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or other user data.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information or user data.**

BouncyBall does not collect information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photographs, device identifiers, or other personal information.

The game does not send any user information to our servers or to third parties over the internet.

## Information Stored Locally

BouncyBall may save your **highest game score** locally on your device.

The saved information consists only of a string containing the numerical value of your highest score. This information is used solely to display and maintain your highest score within the game.

This information:

* is stored only on your device;
* is not transmitted over the internet;
* is not accessible to us;
* is not shared with third parties; and
* does not contain personal information.

If you uninstall BouncyBall or clear its local application data, this locally stored information may be deleted.

## Internet Access

BouncyBall does not transmit user data over the internet.

Any internet connectivity provided by the device or operating system is not used by the game to collect or transmit personal information.

## Third-Party Services

BouncyBall does not use third-party services to collect, process, or share user data.

## Children's Privacy

BouncyBall does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Since the game does not collect personal information, no personal information is knowingly collected from users of any age.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose user data to third parties because we do not collect user data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices of BouncyBall change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe those practices. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
